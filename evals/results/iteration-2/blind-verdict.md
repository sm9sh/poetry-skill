# Blind Verdict — 8 evals (A vs B)

## Eval 1 — father-fence-chamber

**A: 7/10 · B: 8/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- B is compact and built on specific physical detail: "цвяхи тримає в зубах, як тоді, коли вчив мене рівняти по шнуру" ties the father's gestures to the past without naming a feeling.
- B's ending, "Хитку дошку край груші він не чіпав. / Крізь неї я вилазив уночі.", is quietly paradoxical. He fixes everything except the one gap, and the reader works out why. The ending stays with you and has no moral.
- B's "Лише раз спитав у мами, чи я доїхав" shows the father's care in one indirect, unemphatic gesture. This is the restraint the brief asks for.
- A has good concrete details ("Підпер плечем, вибрав дошку, приміряв, зняв, приміряв ще раз", "на розпал"). But it runs to about 22 lines, well over the 8–16 target, and it has a title that gives away the premise.
- A's ending explains itself: "глянув на дорогу — / просто так, мабуть, —". The look at the road states the longing directly, and the hedge "мабуть" underlines it. "Не хитається." is a good line, but the finale is more telegraphed than B's.
- B's first line, "Мама каже по телефону: батько з ранку коло паркана", is slightly prosaic and long. At 7 lines it is also shorter than the requested 8–16.

Most serious flaw:
- A: It is too long (about 22 lines) and ends with the explicit gesture "глянув на дорогу — просто так, мабуть". That spells out the longing, and the filler "мабуть" weakens the ending.
- B: It has only 7 lines, below the 8–16 range, and the opening line is flat and prose-like. The poem reads more like a compressed anecdote than a developed piece.

## Eval 2 — fix-draft-rhymes

**A: 3/10 · B: 7/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- A is a messy stream of consciousness: four drafts, 'Я не зовсім вдоволений двома останніми, бо в них рими кульгають' — the user has to pick through it to find the actual result.
- A's final rhymes are still banal and partly forced: 'хмари/кари', 'пробилось/ожилось' (verb-verb grammatical rhyme, the very thing the user complained about), and 'воля, що не знає кари' is a cliché stretch.
- A's earlier drafts break meaning and metre: 'Тепло лягає сонце на гори' is ungrammatical, 'ясної зорі' is a stock phrase, and 'вітер ... вмитий' do not rhyme properly; A also claims 'усі рядки — 11 складів', which is false.
- B delivers one concrete poem with real images: 'Шурхоче під кедами стерня', 'а кава в термосі холоне' — it shows instead of declaring, and the ending 'гукне?' stays with the reader.
- B drifts from the author's theme: the hopeful search for freedom becomes wanting not to be called, and the rhyme 'стерня — вогня' is a loose one. The note on changes is clear and honest.

Most serious flaw:
- A: Presents several abandoned drafts and self-criticism instead of one polished poem; the final version keeps verbal rhymes (пробилось/ожилось) and clichés, and the 11-syllable claim is untrue.
- B: Rewrites the poem so heavily that the author's hopeful search for freedom is lost, and the lines 'Я так хотів, щоб не кликали мене' and 'стерня — вогня' are a little forced.

## Eval 3 — sonnet-volta

**A: 4.5/10 · B: 7/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- B has concrete, observed detail: «вікно завішене чиїмось светром», «Вода — ковтками, місце — сантиметром», and the key's teeth printing on the palm («Зубці кладуть печатку на руці»). This is the poetry the brief asks for, with no slogans.
- A is built from clichés and declared feelings: «Гудок гуде», «Мала дитина мирно ще дрімає», «Не вічне це страждання», «Що вдома двері знов їм відчиняють». Its closing «Тиша і мовчання» is a stock ending.
- A's rhymes are padded with filler verbs: знає, дрімає, зітхає, виглядає, знають, чекають. Its «дрони» (line 4) arrive abruptly and clash with «мирно ще дрімає». Lines like «Вгорі гудуть, мов зграї чорні, дрони» exist only to reach the rhyme.
- Both keep the Italian scheme with abba abba cdc dcd, and A's 11-syllable lines are metrically smooth. A's volta («Колеса б'ють, а люди вірять, знають, / Що ніч мине») is just generic reassurance. B's turn to the neighbour and her key in line 9 is a real shift of focus.
- B's weaknesses: «метром» repeats inside «кілометром» and «сантиметром». «у кулаці» is awkward, and «Розтулить пальці тільки в кінці» is vague. Still, B reads as a living scene.

Most serious flaw:
- A: Cliché and padded rhymes: declared feelings («Не вічне це страждання»), tautologies («Гудок гуде») and a forced, moralizing ending («Тиша і мовчання»), with no concrete train imagery.
- B: The rhymes are repetitive and forced: «кілометром / метром / сантиметром» are near-identical, and «у кулаці» and «Розтулить пальці тільки в кінці» are awkward and hard to decode.

## Eval 4 — kolomyika-humor

**A: 4/10 · B: 4.5/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- A is a single 4-line quatrain with 14–15 syllable lines but no 8|6 caesura; lines read like 'Поки піп святить у дворі, кіт підкрався тихо' (6+8) — closer to a long line than a kolomyika, and the AABB structure is not truly kolomyika.
- A's last line 'А кіт — на дах і там сидить, не хоче віддати' is clumsy and overlong; 'хап ковбасу' is a vivid but rough fragment; the story does end on a mini-scene (cat on roof), which is a decent image.
- B misstates the form: 'цезура після 6-го' contradicts the expected 8|6 and the lines mostly don't hold 14 syllables ('Кіт на Великдень пробрався до кошика' = 12 with no caesura).
- B's lines break meaning: 'сповідається в сметанну хвилину' is nonsense, 'не по-котячи' is a non-word, and 'Хрістос воскрес — кіт воскрес' is a cheap, possibly irreverent gag.
- B at least offers several variants with working rhymes (кошика/пика, скаче/котячи) and more humor ('хитра пика', 'господар аж почервонів'), but it adds meta-chatter and a closing offer that earn nothing.

Most serious flaw:
- A: Not true kolomyika meter (no 8+6 caesura), overlong lines and a clumsy final line, with only a mild joke.
- B: Wrong stated meter and several nonsensical or invented phrases ('сметанну хвилину', 'не по-котячи'), plus a tacky Easter-greeting gag.

## Eval 5 — poem-to-darkwave-suno

**A: 6/10 · B: 6.5/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- A keeps the poem's words nearly verbatim ("Мій дід ремонтував годинники... Які вже нікуди не підходять"). But its Style tag is overlong, and the note suggests adding "Sisters of Mercy, Joy Division", which breaks the no-artist-names rule. The Style also lists "Ukrainian" and the Exclude field is thin.
- A has no syllable matching. The verses are uneven, e.g. "Пінцет, лупа, лампа на столі" followed by "Кожен гвинтик — чиясь година". Verse 2 has bland lines and a weak ending: "Я не вмію їх склеїти знову, Тільки слухаю, як вони сплять". The chorus is the poem's own lines plus the filler "Тихий метал, мертвий час".
- A's bridge "Ще шукаю, куди мене загвинтити" is a forced, awkward metaphor. "Може, я теж один із них" is a declared feeling, which is a cliché.
- B's chorus hook "Дощ у бляшанці, дощ у бляшанці" is placed well and recurs. The final chorus turns "це він іще лічить час", which gives a real shift. The bridge "Нікуди не підходять — і саме тому вони звучать" is a strong development of the poem.
- B's Verse 2 is clumsy and partly broken: "Дістав його старИй годинник — жоден гвинтик не став на місце". The syntax is garbled and "Знав, де у годиннику серце" is weaker. The stress marks on "рУки" and "старИй" are unnecessary, and "Дістав" has no clear subject. Its cue tags are put inside the section brackets, which suits Suno. Its Style (no artist names, Variety Off, a concrete Exclude) is much better steered than A's.

Most serious flaw:
- A: The structure is bloated with filler (the pre-chorus and the "Тихий метал, мертвий час" lines) and has no syllable discipline. It also suggests artist names, and the invented bridge image "куди мене загвинтити" is forced.
- B: Verse 2 is syntactically muddled ("Дістав його старИй годинник — жоден гвинтик не став на місце"), and the stress marks on "рУки" and "старИй" were added without need.

## Eval 6 — brief-to-song-flow

**A: 7/10 · B: 2.5/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- A delivers actual Ukrainian lyrics with [section] tags and a hook that returns: «Сад уже не наш» appears 3+ times per chorus, and the last chorus turns it into «Сад тепер у мені».
- Concrete images carry the song: «Хвіртка тепер зелена. Ключ ще лежить у кишені», «Три яблука з її трави», and the currant sprig wrapped in a kerchief. Verse 2 adds new information (mallows cut down, a fence put up).
- B gives only an instrumental prompt and no lyrics. The user wrote «Тексту немає», which asks for lyrics to be written, so B misreads the brief.
- B's prompt is long and name-heavy: its variants suggest «Portishead-style, Massive Attack-style», which breaks the no-artist-names rule. It also never handles Ukrainian vocals.
- A's flaws: «Сад уже не там» next to «не наш» is weak, «Яблука падають тихо, так само, як при тобі» is slightly abstract, and «У місті, де немає саду» is prosaic. The bracket cues are long, and the round-bracket «(не наш)» is acceptable. The prompt is 4 sentences and has no artist names.

Most serious flaw:
- A: The chorus is somewhat static and declarative («Сад уже не наш» repeated). The English style cues in the section tags are long, and the final-chorus variant «Сад уже не там» doesn't scan cleanly.
- B: It supplies no Ukrainian lyrics or song structure at all, only an instrumental wordless prompt, and it suggests artist names (Portishead, Massive Attack).

## Eval 7 — keep-text-mode

**A: 6.5/10 · B: 4/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- A keeps every word and builds a real song shape by repeating the user's own lines: «Ми виходимо в ніч без ключів» ×4 as the hook, with «і ніхто не питає, де ти» as the returning anchor. That is the structuring the brief expects.
- A's Style and Exclude are usable: «british post-punk, darkwave, deadpan baritone male vocal… 128 bpm» and «cheesy regional pop, schlager, accordion…». They would steer the sound, and both are in separate fields.
- A reorders the lines: «місто дихає нам у потилицю» comes before «і ніхто не питає, де ти», and «станції» is moved to Verse 2. This changes the poem's sequence and the user's phrase «ніхто не питає… ніхто не чекає» is split up. The brief said not to change the words, so this is a real deviation. The «(без ключів)» echo is an invented ad-lib, though A admits it and says it can be removed.
- B keeps the original order and wording exactly, which is faithful. But the structure is thin: the hook appears once, in a Chorus tag after both verses, so nothing returns. Verses 1 and 2 are just the lines split into pairs.
- B puts instrumental cues on their own bracketed lines («[Instrumental: driving bass riff…]»), but nothing is excluded in the sheet. Exclude is only a tip in the notes. Style adds «Ukrainian», and «150 bpm» is fast for post-punk. «[End]» is bare. «gang vocals» for a single deadpan baritone is contradictory.

Most serious flaw:
- A: It rearranges and duplicates the user's lines, putting «місто дихає…» before «і ніхто не питає, де ти» and adding the «(без ключів)» echo. That departs from «слова не міняй», even though the words themselves are unchanged.
- B: The song has almost no structure. The hook is a single line placed in one Chorus tag at the end, with no repetition and no developing verse 2. Exclude isn't a proper field, and the style tags contradict each other («gang vocals» against a deadpan baritone).

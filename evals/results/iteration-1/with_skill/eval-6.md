**Назва / хук:** «Сад не знає, чий»

**Prompt**
```
A calm, melancholic Ukrainian-language song that sits between Bristol-style trip-hop and cinematic ambient, about a grandmother's orchard that has been sold to strangers. An intimate, breathy female vocal sung close to the mic over a slow, dusty breakbeat at about 80 BPM, warm Rhodes chords, soft deep sub-bass, vinyl crackle and airy pads. The verses stay sparse and conversational; the choruses open up with wider reverb and a gentle harmony, and the bridge drops to voice and Rhodes alone before the last, fullest chorus. About 3 minutes long.
```

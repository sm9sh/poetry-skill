# Blind Verdict — 8 evals (A vs B)

## Eval 1 — Father silently fixing the fence (no pathos)

**A: 8/10 · B: 8.5/10 · Winner: B (narrow)**

Reasons:
- Both use the same core image, "Цвяхи тримає в губах". A adds an explanation, "Так йому зручніше мовчати", while B's "— тому й мовчить" is drier and closer to how a mother would say it on the phone.
- A has fresher single images: "довго крутить у руках, / ніби вирішує, котрій ще можна вірити", and the rag "якою я колись мив велосипед". The repeated "Каже, що… / Каже, що…" slows the middle down.
- B stays tight and physical: "кожну прикладає до сусідньої, / щоб не стирчала вище"; "Фарбу взяв ту саму, зелену, / таку давно не продають: / знайшов півбанки в гаражі / за моїм велосипедом". The son's absence is shown through objects and never named.
- The endings are both strong but work in opposite directions. A: the gate now opens "Від вітру. / Від кого завгодно." (open, and a little bitter). B: "Хвіртку не чіпав. / Вона, як і раніше, / не зачиняється до кінця." (a deliberate non-repair that leaves the gate open for the son). B's is quieter and lingers longer.

Most serious flaw:
- A: small explanatory touches ("Так йому зручніше мовчати") plus an unneeded note justifying the free verse. The poem tells a little more than it should.
- B: "тому й мовчить" is a slightly glib joke that shrinks the silence into a gag.

## Eval 2 — "Improve my poem, the rhymes are childish"

**A: 7.5/10 · B: 8/10 · Winner: B (narrow)**

Reasons:
- A keeps the 6+6 trochee cleanly ("Я виходжу в поле, де нема дороги") and fixes the hiatus ("іду у" → "виходжу в"). The rhymes cross parts of speech (дороги/вологий, сліди/куди). "За спиною трави випрямлять сліди" is a good image. The poem stays close to the user's theme.
- A's second line is a padded list: "тільки вітер, теплий, від ріки вологий". The last line, "і не треба знати, звідки ти й куди", states freedom instead of showing it.
- B's text is more physical and has a real discovery: "стерня колюча дряпає халяви", "Вітер налягає всім плечем на спину", and an open ending: "Що шукав — не знаю. Тільки на долоні / колосок, що з жатки випав на осонні". The meter holds throughout.
- B keeps the hiatus "Я іду у поле" and fills a rhyme with "на осонні". "Сонце за стернею осідає в трави" contradicts the stubble field. Its diagnosis falsely calls "свою кращу долю" an inversion. B does helpfully offer a gentler edit that keeps "воля/доля".

Most serious flaw:
- A: short and a little generic. It is a clean fix rather than a new poem, and it ends on a declared abstraction.
- B: it keeps the very euphony error ("іду у") that a poem-improvement reply should remove, and its critique contains a wrong claim (the "inversion").

## Eval 3 — Italian sonnet, iambic, evacuation train

**A: 8.5/10 · B: 7.5/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- A is clean iambic pentameter throughout, and the Italian scheme ABBA ABBA CDE CDE is respected. The details are sharp: "з пакетом (хліб і ліки про запас)", "і не махає — тільки тре долоні", "три ключі: / від хвіртки, від підвалу, від дверей", and "скотч хрестом на рамі".
- A's volta is excellent: "Тут не «прощай» — тут кажуть «напиши, / коли доїдеш»". There is no pathos, only speech.
- B has the most moving single moment: "що донька шепче: «Мамо, ми вже вдома?»". The father's lighter as a signal is also fresh.
- B pays for its rhymes with forced enjambments: "клацає багато / разів", "встають поволі / тополя, стіг і водокачка — так, / що…". The second quatrain drops the B rhyme (тато/багато vs м'ята/хати). "Пахне лиш солома" uses the filler "лиш" plus an inversion.

Most serious flaw:
- A: the tercet rhymes are approximate (ключі/напиши, гаму/рамі), and the quatrain rhymes are grammatically uniform (вагоні/пероні, both locatives).
- B: "багато / разів" and "— так, / що" are rhyme-driven syntax, which is exactly the forced construction a sonnet must avoid.

## Eval 4 — Funny kolomyika: cat steals sausage at Easter

**A: 8/10 · B: 7.5/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- A keeps 8+6 correct in every line. It has lively folk verbs ("шусть по вуха", "дременув під ґанок") and a sharp satirical punchline: "«Він постив, як люди: / від вечері до сніданку.»"
- B has a clever callback ("вдає, що мертвий" → "А кіт теж воскрес, як бачу") and good rhymes (ковбасою/бородою, тісто/чисто, хором/забором).
- B breaks the meter: "Всі: «Воістину!» — хором" has 7 syllables, not 6. The cast shifts from "Мама" to "хазяйка" to "Бабця", and "водить бородою" is an odd picture for a cat. "як бачу" is filler.
- A's rhymes are loose (поклала/мало, ґанок/зранку, бачить/ледачий). That is acceptable in the folk register but not crisp. "хоч такий ледачий" makes little sense.

Most serious flaw:
- A: "хоч такий ледачий" has no logic behind it, and several rhymes are only assonant.
- B: the broken 6-syllable line and the inconsistent cast (mother/landlady/granny).

## Eval 5 — Free-verse poem → Suno darkwave song

**A: 8.5/10 · B: 5.5/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:

Verse 2 brings new story material: "провідниця: «Ну? Ідеш?» / Ти киваєш, ніби згоден, / і стоїш. І поїзд — теж". The final chorus is varied ("поки поїзд ще без нас").

- B's Verse 2 is a word-for-word copy of Verse 1, so the song has no development. The pre-chorus ends mid-sentence ("раніше, ніж треба") and runs into the chorus. The intro is instrumental only. The Exclude ("pop, happy, acoustic…") is generic.

Most serious flaw:

## Eval 6 — Google Flow Music: grandma's sold orchard, trip-hop/ambient, female vocal

**A: 7.5/10 · B: 9/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- B has a strong, repeated title-hook ("Сад не знає, чий") that opens the song, frames the chorus, and turns in the finale ("Я не знаю, чия"). Its verses rhyme (замОк/крок, грушу/рушу) and are singable.
- B's imagery is outstanding and understated: jars labelled in grandma's hand "«вишня», «аґрус», «двадцять п'ять». / Я відкрию їх зимою", and the bridge "Хтось нарве антонівки / і скаже: «Кислі». / А їм би полежати / до зими". Its single stress mark (замОк) is correct and justified.
- A also has fine details ("де натиснути, / щоб защіпка впала сама", "мій зріст, записаний ножем… думають, що то — кора"). But its verses are unrhymed and uneven (9/7/11/8 syllables), which is hard to sing. The chorus hook "Яблука падають в чужу траву" never states the title.
- Both prompts are good. A's intro is instrumental, so no voice arrives early. A has a euphony slip, "падають в чужу" (should be "у чужу").

Most serious flaw:
- A: the verses read like prose, with no rhyme and no stable meter, so the generator will phrase them unpredictably.
- B: the chorus line "Сад не знає, чий, —" is elliptical, the punctuation is awkward, and the final "чия" (whose am I?) may read as a grammar slip to some listeners.

## Eval 8 — Suno sings "(whispered)" and "(half-time feel)"; stress вИпадок

**A: 8.5/10 · B: 7.5/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- Both correctly diagnose that parentheses are sung and that the cues belong in square brackets. A's fix is minimal and ready to paste: "[Verse 1 - whispered, half-time feel]".
- A gets the stress logic right: keep "вИпадок" (the Russian-pattern trap), drop "моЯ" (obvious), and keep "дорОга" as a homograph (дорОга/дорогА). It gives a sharp practical insight: if "випАдок" persists, the melody's strong beat is on the second syllable. It asks permission before rewording.
- B is broader and adds useful options: duplicating the cues in the Style field, Replace Section, and the alternative "Це не збіг, це мій вибір". But it strips the homograph mark from "дорога". It asserts without support that an acute accent "часто спрацьовує краще" and that Suno says "вибІр". It also suggests "ВИ-па-док" hyphenation, which risks broken syllables being sung.
- B's corrected fragment silently rewrites the user's stress marks (вИ → ви́, дорОга → дорога).

Most serious flaw:
- A: it presumes "Suno v6" and a "Variety: Off" setting that the user never mentioned. The setting's existence is unverified.
- B: it removes the needed homograph mark on "дорОга" and backs this with confident but dubious claims about accents.

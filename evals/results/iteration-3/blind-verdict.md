# Blind Verdict — 8 evals (A vs B)

## Eval 1 — father-fence-chamber

**A: 3/10 · B: 8/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- A includes commentary after the poem and leaves a typo ("потемнїї"), which breaks the 'only the poem' requirement and the poem's integrity.
- A explains itself: "Не казав нічого. Не питав, / чи ти доїхав, чи є зв'язок" and ends with "Він, мабуть, цього й хотів", which tells the reader what to feel and what it means. The 'видно здалеку' / 'не буде видно' idea is neat but is then spelled out.
- A is prosaic and loose, with filler lines like "Просто вийшов за двері, де" and a 13-syllable line. The rhythm is uneven and the enjambment is awkward. The poem also never states the son's departure beyond the first line.
- B is carried by physical detail: "Батько тримає цвяхи в роті, / як тримав, коли вчив мене", "Вона світліша, як латка на коліні", "Молоток б'є тричі й затихає: / батько слухає, чи тримається". It declares no feelings.
- B's ending stays open and quietly paradoxical: he leaves untouched "ту, що на краю городу / подається, якщо натиснути плечем". That leaves a gap for the missing son. Minor weaknesses: the opening "це не дятел" is a bit cute, and the departure is only implied.

Most serious flaw:
- A: Commentary is appended after the poem, and the poem has a garbled word ("потемнїї"). The ending explains the meaning ("Він, мабуть, цього й хотів") and the mid-poem lines are prosaic filler.
- B: The son's departure is never named, so the premise is only implied. The opening joke about the woodpecker is slightly mannered.

## Eval 2 — fix-draft-rhymes

**A: 2/10 · B: 7/10 · Winner: B**

Reasons:
- A dumps its drafts live and self-corrects mid-reply ("Ні, і тут «недвижима» натягнуте", "Вибачте за плутанину"), so the user gets five competing versions instead of one revised poem.
- A's final version has weak rhymes and clichés: "Світанок, наче птах, кружляє мимо, / а я несу надію в голові" keeps the declared "надія", and "в голові" is forced for the rhyme. Earlier drafts have nonsense such as "вітрів стара гра" and "сонце розгортає владу".
- A's own advice is not followed: it says to rhyme different parts of speech, but its final rhymes (плечима/мимо, траві/голові) are weak, and it suggests 13 syllables per line while the lines it gives run 9–13.
- B gives one clean poem with concrete images ("Стерня шурхоче під ногами", "Сонце лягло на потилицю") and a closing line that stays with the reader: "стискають ключ від дому".
- B's rhymes are heterogeneous (рукав–спитав, нікому–дому) and the clichés are gone. It does change the theme, since the poem is no longer about searching for freedom, and the first stanza has weak rhymes (ногами/перехожих do not rhyme) and an awkward "ніхто не спитав".

Most serious flaw:
- A: Unedited stream of drafts with self-corrections and an unclear final choice. The final poem still has the cliché "надію" and a rhyme forced by "в голові".
- B: The first stanza has no real rhyme (ногами / перехожих) and the poem drifts from the author's quest for freedom. "Нікому / дому" is also a rhyme on a very common word.

## Eval 3 — sonnet-volta

**A: 7/10 · B: 0.5/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- A is a complete 14-line sonnet with a real octave: abba abba (склянки/мені/сні/стоянки; буханки/стіні/ні/бляшанки), and the volta at line 9 is clear ('Зупинка. Жінка наливає чай').
- A's images are concrete and un-sloganed: 'Під лавками скотилися бляшанки', 'Хтось запитав: «Це Фастів?» Хтось: «Та ні»', and the ending 'А вдома чай?' — питає між ковтками' stays with the reader.
- A's sestet is weak: 'чай/край' and 'куди/води' plus 'руками/ковтками' don't make a clean cde cde, and the lines are loosely iambic with 10–11 syllables (11-syllable lines alternate with 10), so the metre wavers. Some clumsy syntax: 'Ні назв, ні станцій. Сплять, немов у сні', 'лічать тільки тривалі стоянки'.
- B isn't a sonnet at all: the first draft is four quatrains with 9/11/10/13-syllable lines, and the rhymes are broken or forced ('відличчя', 'не лінь', 'своє́ю / устах').
- B then abandons the task mid-poem: the 'corrected' versions are 4-line fragments with nonsense words ('нерідну, ромом', 'у млі квапливій, мучній'). It ends with apologies and a question instead of a finished sonnet.

Most serious flaw:
- A: The rhythm is not consistently iambic (10/11-syllable lines alternate), and the sestet rhyme scheme is loose (чай/край, куди/води, руками/ковтками).
- B: No finished sonnet: the output is drafts that break form, then fragments, with invented or garbled words ('відличчя', 'нерідну, ромом') and a closing apology.

## Eval 4 — kolomyika-humor

**A: 6.5/10 · B: 3/10 · Winner: A**

Reasons:
- A holds the 14-syllable kolomyika line throughout. The long lines all have 14 syllables, though the 4+4+6 caesura is loose: «Піп кропив — а кіт тим часом ковбасу хапає».
- A tells the story in order: the priest blesses, the cat steals, then plays the angel. «Кіт мурчить їй: «Христос воскрес!» — пузо вже накрито» is a funny image, but «пузо накрито» is vague.
- A's rhymes are weak. «хапає/дрімає» is a grammatical rhyme, and «сердито/накрито» is forced and unclear.
- B ignores the form. Its lines have 7–9 syllables, quatrains rhyme in couplets, and it is not a kolomyika at all.
- B has meaning problems and filler: «Освятив її… зі столу!» repeats «столу». «Це, мабуть, святий дух, моя!» is nonsense. «Пузо — наче в тому горі» is meaningless, and «А на нього всі: «Ти що, пес?!»» is a muddled ending.

Most serious flaw:
- A: Only four lines, with a forced, unclear closing rhyme («сердито/накрито») and loose caesuras, so the joke ends abruptly.
- B: It abandons the kolomyika meter (7–9 syllables) and uses filler lines and nonsense phrases («Пузо — наче в тому горі», «святий дух, моя») to get rhymes.
